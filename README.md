# Erif: Unyielding Flame

A small HTML5 Canvas boss-rush game. You play a guttering flame working through eight lieutenant trials before facing Erif itself — with a longer, Hard-only true ending for anyone who clears all eight without giving up their embers.

No build step, no dependencies — it's plain HTML/CSS/JS.

**Play it here:** https://lestructo.github.io/Erif/

## Playing locally

Just open `index.html` in a browser. That's the whole setup.

## Controls

- **WASD** — move
- **Arrow keys** (or **I/J/K/L**) — aim your shield
- **Space** — confirm / advance dialogue / attack (where applicable)
- **R** — instantly restart from a loss screen
- **Esc** — pause

## Difficulty

- **Normal** and **Hard** are both available from the title screen.
- Hard mode has its own true ending, unlocked by clearing all eight lieutenants without losing a Ward.
